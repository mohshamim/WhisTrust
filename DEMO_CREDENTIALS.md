## 🔐 **Password Best Practices**

**Current Pattern:** `[Role]@WhistTrust2025`

**Alternative Patterns:**

- `WhistTrust@[Role]2025`
- `Demo[Role]@2025!`
- `[Role]Demo@WT2025`

**Characteristics:**

- ✅ Minimum 12 characters
- ✅ Contains uppercase
- ✅ Contains lowercase
- ✅ Contains special character (@)
- ✅ Contains numbers
- ✅ Easy to remember for demos
- ✅ Professional appearance
